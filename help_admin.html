Admin Stuff

You want to know how to manage accounts and products? I will tell you. Firstly, you must logged into an administrator account. If you are unfamiliar with the account login/registration process, please see the other relevant help section.

You will only be able to see/access the admin sections of the website if you are an admin. Once you are signed into an admin account, you will see the red "Admin" and "Status" sections in the navigation bar. The status simply shows the operational status of the relevant important parts of the website like the database. You can check here if you suspect issues and should find an answer there.

In the admin section is where you get controls. In here, at the top of the page you can enter the information of a new product, includeing the name, description, category, price and the link to the image. Then below you can view all exisiting product and make whatever changes needed, which can include deleting entire products. Finally, you can view all accounts (including your own) and enable/disable any of them. Disabled accounts are not able to login.
